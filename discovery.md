# Week 1 - Initial Discovery 
## 1. Facts
Bookings are currently managed through a couple of methods, email, spreadsheets and informal arrangements.
People do not have a way to see whether equipment is available or not. 
Staff manually check the bookings.
Equipment can be double booked.
Students are not always sure who to contact about equipment.
## 2. Assumptions 
There's no incentive to encourage on time returns/no penalty put in place to discourage late returns.
There is no staff member assigned authority over the system/There is no administrator.
There's no way for people to see if equipment is currently booked or even available. 
There's no concrete method to book and track equipment.
## 3. Unknowns 
The amount of time equipment goes out for. (What's to stop someone from just booking something permanentely?) 
Is there a member of staff for students to talk to in order to inquire about bookings.
The amount of equipment bookable at a time.
Who is the equipment provider.
## 4. Stakeholders
Students: Availability, Easy Booking.
Staff: Equipment for lectures/preparing material.
IT Dep: Status of equipment.
College: Price of equipment/price of repairs.
Security: Possibility of people trying to steal equipment.
# 1 Conflict.
Students may want to take equipment home for study that the security/IT Team do not want to happen as equipment may go lost. 
## 5. Goals
The system should help the college to make sure there is a stable flow of equipment coming back in when equipment is going out.
The system should help the college to assign a way to measure the status of individual equipment, seeing if a certain piece is available, stolen, currently in or out of the college.
The system should help the college to keep records of who's taking what equipment, making sure that one person isn't constantly booking out equipment.
## 6. Scope
# In Scope:
Lack of communication causing the availability of equipment to be unknown.
The status of someone (e.g student or a member of staff) changing the amount/quality of equipment they are allowed to borrow.
No way of knowing if someone is always returning equipment late/not returning equipment at all.

## 7. Candidate Requirements
# Functional Requirements:
The system shall allow an authorised user to request equipment for a specified date/time.
The system will have measures in place to penalise people for not returning equipment in time. 
The system will prevent double booking from occuring.
# Non-Functional Requirements:
The system will support accessible use.
The system will verify who someone is before bringing them to the booking page.
