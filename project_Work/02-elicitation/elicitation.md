#1. Stakeholders
Stakeholder: Organisers
Role: Assigning Volunteers to events, and making sure they have the correct skillsets
Direct user?: Yes
Possible care: The current way of organising Volunteers is time consuming, so a possibility would be making the process more efficient by letting organisers see Volunteers who have labelled themselves as available
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stakeholder: Volunteers
Role: Working on activities based on what the organisers have assigned them 
Direct user?: Possibly (Currently not 100% sure)
Possible care: They need a clear way to display to Organisers whether they are available to work or if they are not currently available.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stakeholder: Local Community
Role: The local community benefit from having Volunteers work on activities that are going on around the area 
Direct user?: No
Possible care: They would benefit from the process of organising Volunteers being sped up and made more efficient
