My Prototype is displaying FR01 - The system should have a way to allow Organizers to match Volunteers to an activity, Showing the process of 
assigning Volunteers to activities, it goes through the process of seeing the current Activities that are needing assistance,
filtering volunteers by if they are available and then filtering them by qualifications (In this case being a community bake sale, it is filtered to someone with baking experience)
This has also given me the idea for CR02 - Volunteers having a displayable profile that will allow organizers to view their name, qualifications as well as 
a short self written description about themselves, I have marked my 04 drawio diagram as candidate for this reason.
