## Chosen Requirement: 
FR02 - The system should allow organizers to specify when a certain activity needs people with certain skills.
## User Story: 
As an Event Organizer, I want to tag an activity with specific required skills as to specify to Volunteers which activities require a person who has a certain set of skills.
## Acceptance Criteria:
When an event with specific qualifications needed is posted, these qualifications should be viewable by Volunteers, clearly outlined and allow those certain Volunteers to work on this activity.
## Definition of Done 
The activities that require certain qualifications should only allow Volunteers with the required Qualifications to be available to apply themselves to this work, due to the nature of the requirements, 100% of the time this should work.

## Chosen Requirement: 
FR04 - The system should give an option for Volunteers to drop out of an activity, as well as a notification to Organizers when a Volunteer drops out of an activity.
## User Story: 
As a Volunteer, I have been applied to an activity but something has come up in my life, meaning I wish to drop out and notify the Organisers of my dropping out of the activity. 
## Acceptance Criteria:
When this Volunteers clicks the button to drop out of an activity, they will be put off of the list of participants and will notify the Organisers of this dropping out
## Definition of Done 
When the "Drop out" Button is clicked, the account will be put off the list and there will be a notification sent to the Organisers.
