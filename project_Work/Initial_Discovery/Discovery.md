# Initial Discovery

## 1. Facts 
Organisers
- Currently matching Volunteers to activities take up a lot of the organisers time 
- There's no concrete way for organisers to know if a Volunteer is available or not 
-  Organisers need to know which Volunteers are attending which activities

Volunteers
- Not always sure what Activities need help
- Get matched to activities manually by Organisers
- Their information is not stored in the one place, but stored across several databases

Other facts
- There's no current system for changes and cancellations to be communicated
- Some Activities need a person with a particular set of skills or a certain level of experience
- Important: Consider the accessibility of the program and the privacy of others data 


## 2. Assumptions 
There's a lack of communication between Organisers and Volunteers
The organisers spend considerable time trying to match certain activities to someone with certain skills
Volunteers don't have a way to know what activities need attention
Volunteers can help with an activity that needs help without being matched by an Organiser


## 3. Unknowns
What Information of the Volunteers is stored? 
Is it always needed for an Organiser to apply a Volunteer to an activity? (E.g. Could the Organisers pay more attention to bigger jobs while the Volunteers would apply themselves to the smaller tasks)
How do they currently handle cancellations and changes?
What's the process of someone becoming a volunteer? Are they provided training?

## 4. Stakeholders
Organisers: Keeping Track of volunteer, Assigning Volunteers
Volunteers:  Knowing what activities are available, knowing what 
Local Community:  Volunteers benefit them greatly, get to participate in organised events


### Possible conflict between Stakeholders
The organisers may want more control over the allocation of work and the volunteers may want to assign themselves

## 5. Goals
Make a software with an interface that 90% of trained users should be able to navigate without issue

## 6. Scope


### In Scope:
There's no clear way to see what activities are in need of assistants
Some activities want volunteers with a certain skillset
Volunteers have no way to show if they are currently available to work or not 
The process of matching volunteers to activities is time consuming for Organisers

### Out of Scope:
A way to view what jobs are near you 

## 7. Candidate requirements


## Functional Requirements
The system shall allow Organisers to match volunteers with activities that they think suits best
The system shall allow Volunteers to display when they are available to work
The system shall allow Volunteers to see which activities are currently in need of more assistance
The system shall allows Volunteers of certain skillsets to apply themselves to those activities needing them

## Non-Functional Requirements
One location to store the needed information of the Volunteers in opposition to the current scattered system 
A more time efficient process for Organisers to assign volunteers to activities
